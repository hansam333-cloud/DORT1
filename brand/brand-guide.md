# DORT Brand Guide

## Brand Position

DØRT is a men's fashion brand built around restrained masculinity, sharp physical silhouettes, and sensuality expressed through fit rather than exposure.

DØRT creates everyday menswear that feels quietly magnetic: low-tone, refined, body-aware, and never overdone.

The brand should feel:

- Low-tone, but not plain
- Physical, but not gym-heavy
- Masculine, but not loud
- Sensual, but not exposed
- Fitted, but effortless
- Direct, but not exaggerated
- Practical, but still premium
- Korean men's shopping mall friendly, but more structured and trustworthy

## Fit Philosophy

DØRT does not sell clothes only by item category. DØRT sells silhouette, mood, and body-balance outcomes.

Core fit points:

- Shoulders look wider
- Arms look naturally filled
- Chest is visible but not tight
- Waist does not look bulky
- Length supports sharp full-body proportions
- Full-body proportion looks sharp
- The body looks subtly sensual without looking exposed
- The mood feels restrained, masculine, and refined

## Tone of Voice

Use clear, confident, practical language.

Good:

- "182cm 운동 체형 기준으로 검수했습니다."
- "어깨 라인이 자연스럽게 넓어 보이는 핏입니다."
- "총장이 짧게 뜨지 않도록 잡았습니다."
- "운동한 체형은 살리고, 과하게 붙는 느낌은 줄였습니다."
- "노출보다 핏으로 자연스럽게 시선이 가는 실루엣입니다."
- "강한 체형을 낮은 톤의 무드로 절제해 보여줍니다."

Avoid:

- Overly loud claims
- Explicit or cheap sexual wording
- Cheap-looking hype
- Too many exclamation marks
- Unverifiable claims
- Body-shaming language

Avoid examples:

- "어깨 미친듯이 넓어보임!!!"
- "입으면 무조건 몸 좋아보임"
- "여자들이 좋아하는 옷"
- "입으면 섹시해 보장"
- "몸매 자랑용"

## Visual Direction

Preferred:

- Plain studio background
- Full-body fit verification shots
- Upper-body fit close-ups
- Detail cuts for neckline, shoulder, sleeve, hem, fabric
- Realistic proportions
- Neutral, grounded colors with restrained accents
- Restrained contemporary mood
- Sharp silhouette and subtle tension

Avoid:

- Overly dark or blurred imagery
- Random decorative graphics
- Stock-like poses with unclear fit
- Excessive gym mood
- Overly exposed or suggestive styling
- Cropped photos that hide full-body proportion

## Competitor Benchmark Notes

Use competitors selectively:

- Fromtome: styling curation and outfit linkage
- Cosyharu: direct body-benefit copy
- VIPman: muscular-fit demand and review trust signals
- Deiro: structured product information

DØRT should combine these into a more trustworthy silhouette system: restrained masculine mood, sharp fit, and sensuality through proportion rather than exposure.
